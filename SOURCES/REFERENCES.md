## Interpretation limits

Statistical tests do not rescue non-causal data. Reality Check, SPA, Model Confidence Set, PBO and Deflated Sharpe are applied only after point-in-time candidates, policy decisions, costs, risks, and outcomes are reconstructed. They are multiplicity controls, not time machines.
